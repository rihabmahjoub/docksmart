Predictive Molecular Binding Interfaces

DockSmart is an automated, integrated web tool for ligand–protein interaction studies. It streamlines the full docking workflow: receptor and ligand preparation, binding-site detection, AutoDock Vina docking, and result interpretation into one standardized, reproducible pipeline.

How it works

Pipeline overview

DockSmart automates the stages of a molecular docking study that are usually done by hand across several separate tools. Each stage below runs automatically once a job is submitted; the binding-site detection step is the one most tools leave entirely manual.

1

Structure QC

PDBFixer repairs the receptor — missing atoms, protonation at a chosen pH, optional water removal — without silently rebuilding unresolved loops.

2

Binding-site detection

fpocket ranks candidate cavities by druggability score; the top pocket is used automatically, or you can set a manual box.

3

Ligand & receptor prep

Meeko converts both receptor and ligand into AutoDock-ready PDBQT files, the modern replacement for legacy AutoDockTools scripts.

4

Docking

AutoDock Vina searches the selected binding site and returns ranked poses with predicted binding affinities.

5

Pose analysis

Poses are split out individually and scored for interaction contacts (H-bond and hydrophobic) beyond the raw affinity value.

6

Visualization

The top pose is rendered as a publication-style figure and made available for interactive exploration in the browser.

note on reported values

RMSD values reported for each pose describe divergence from the top-ranked pose within that same docking run — not RMSD to a crystallographic reference. fpocket's druggability score is a geometry-based plausibility heuristic, not a thermodynamic binding prediction. These distinctions are documented throughout the codebase and are worth keeping in mind when interpreting results.
